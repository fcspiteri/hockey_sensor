HOCKEY SENSOR REV A — TURNKEY 4-LAYER PCBA FABRICATION & ASSEMBLY NOTES
=======================================================================

1. BARE-BOARD PCB SPECIFICATIONS
--------------------------------
- Board Outline:        38.00 mm x 28.00 mm (rounded corners R = 3.00 mm on Edge.Cuts)
- Layer Count:          4 copper layers (F.Cu, In1.Cu, In2.Cu, B.Cu)
- Finished Thickness:   0.80 mm +/- 10%
- Base Material:        FR-4 High-Tg (Tg >= 170 C, e.g., Isola 370HR or Shengyi S1000-2M), Er ~ 4.2 at 2.4 GHz
- Copper Weights:
    * L1 (F.Cu):        1.0 oz (35 um) finished
    * L2 (In1.Cu):      0.5 oz (17.5 um) — Solid /GND reference plane (8.0 x 8.0 mm top-right RF keepout)
    * L3 (In2.Cu):      0.5 oz (17.5 um) — /VDD_1V8 power plane + signal routing (8.0 x 8.0 mm top-right RF keepout)
    * L4 (B.Cu):        1.0 oz (35 um) finished — /GND plane + bottom signal escapes (8.0 x 8.0 mm top-right RF keepout)
- Dielectric Stackup:
    * L1 to L2 Prepreg: 0.11 mm (2116 prepreg, Er = 4.2)
    * L2 to L3 Core:    0.47 mm FR-4 core
    * L3 to L4 Prepreg: 0.11 mm (2116 prepreg, Er = 4.2)
- Controlled Impedance:
    * 50 Ohm +/- 10% single-ended microstrip on L1 (F.Cu) referenced to L2 (In1.Cu): 0.20 mm trace width (RF_ANT_OUT, RF_MATCH_MID, RF_ANT_FEED)
    * 90 Ohm +/- 10% differential on USB_D_P / USB_D_N
- Minimum Design Rules:
    * Min Trace / Space:       0.09 mm / 0.09 mm (3.5 mil / 3.5 mil)
    * Min Via Drill / Pad:     0.20 mm drill / 0.40 mm pad
    * Min Hole-to-Copper:      0.20 mm
    * Copper-to-Edge:          0.25 mm
- Surface Finish:       ENIG (Electroless Nickel Immersion Gold, 2-3 uin Au over 120-240 uin Ni)
- Solder Mask:          Matte Black or Green LPI both sides, 0.04 mm max expansion, 0.08 mm min web
- Via Protection:       All vias tented with solder mask (or IPC-4761 Type VII plugged & capped for thermal vias in U1, U2, U7 exposed pads)
- Silkscreen:           White epoxy ink, clipped away from exposed pads/mask openings
- Electrical Test:      100% flying-probe netlist continuity & isolation test against IPC-356 / Gerber netlist

2. SMT ASSEMBLY (PCBA) NOTES
----------------------------
- Assembly Side:        Single-sided TOP (F.Cu) SMT assembly only. Bottom side (B.Cu) must remain completely flat for LiPo battery stackup.
- BOM File:             hockey_sensor_pcba_bom.csv
- Centroid / CPL File:  hockey_sensor_cpl_pick_and_place.csv (units: mm, origin: KiCad board coordinates matching Gerber origin)
- Do Not Populate (DNP):
    * C28 (0.8 pF 0402 RF shunt tuning pad — leave unpopulated for initial Rev A build)
    * J2 (TC2050-IDC-NL Tag-Connect pogo footprint — no physical connector part)
    * BT1 (Solder pads for off-board 3.7 V 250 mAh LiPo cell leads)
    * FID1, FID2, FID3 (Optical fiducials)
    * MH1, MH2, MH3, MH4 (1.50 mm NPTH M1.4 mounting holes)
- X-Ray Inspection:     100% AXI inspection required on U1 (nRF5340-QKAA aQFN-94, 0.40 mm pitch) and U2 (nPM1300-QEAA QFN-32) to verify zero solder bridges and < 25% voiding on central thermal pads.
